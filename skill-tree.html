4 · Shaping the Build

4 sub-skills · most career-defining

Ng's first shaping skill: most software is built in a loop — write code, get feedback, decide the next step — and a skilled engineer drives it at AI velocity with a bias for action. Deciding whether the next step is a quick prototype, an MVP to take to users, more features, or an enterprise-grade investment; shipping in small batches; knowing when to get user/stakeholder feedback vs run a technical experiment; factoring product vision, project stage, feasibility, risk, effort, budget; defining and project-managing key metrics on mature projects. This is where the MVP-vs-careful pace call lives — the tension from Ng's three loops — and it compounds into FDE pricing power.
