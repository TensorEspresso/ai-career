# Part 4 — Calibration Cadence

The quiet failure mode is never testing yourself. This part is the measurement
loop. Real interviews are the only honest measurement of a low-bar environment's
effect on you.

## The cadence

| Frequency | Action |
|---|---|
| Every 3–4 months | One real technical interview (or a paid mock if real is impossible) |
| Within 1 week of each interview | Write the gap list while fresh |
| Monthly | Review `templates/scorecard.md`; feed gaps into daily practice |
| Quarterly | Re-score the checklist in `01-external-bar.md` |

## Using real interviews as data

1. Apply to companies you would genuinely join, at the level above your current
   title (AI engineer / senior).
2. Treat every stage as a free measurement: the recruiter screen tells you your
   market positioning; the take-home tells you your engineering habits; the
   system-design loop tells you your ceiling.
3. Rejected? Ask for feedback, write it down, and bank it. A rejection with a
   gap list is worth more than a pass.
4. Passed at a good company? You now hold two levers: the offer itself, and the
   calibrated knowledge of your market rate for the internal comp conversation.

## Gap tracking

Keep one file (`gap-log.md` in this folder). Format per entry:

```
YYYY-MM-DD — <source: interview loop / mock / project postmortem>
Gap: <specific, e.g. "could not defend p95 latency choice">
Fix: <concrete action mapped to 02-daily-quality-bar.md or swe-fde-curriculum.md phase>
Status: open / closed (date closed)
```

Rule from the interview: every gap found goes straight into Part 2 practice.
Gaps logged and never worked = measurement without movement.

## Decision signals

The signal to make the move — internal role change or external jump — is
converging evidence, not a feeling:

- All rows in the `01-external-bar.md` checklist at 4+.
- At least one interview pass at a company with a real bar.
- The ownership story in `03-external-proof.md` updated with a shipped AI
  system in the last 6 months.

When those three line up:

1. If the internal AI engineering path is real (title + comp band exist): make
   the internal ask, armed with the market number.
2. If it is not (informal leveling, no comp band, low bar persists): move
   externally. The curriculum has done its job; the environment has not.

Counterevidence to watch for — signs the low bar is capturing you:

- Two consecutive quarters with nothing new for the ownership story.
- Scorecard scores flat or falling.
- You cannot name one rigorous standard you enforced at work in 90 days.
