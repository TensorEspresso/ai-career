# Bar Curriculum — External Standards in a Low-Bar Job

This set answers one question: how do you protect your market value when your
current company has a low or non-existent engineering bar?

The rest of this repo (skill tree, FDE curriculum, growth plan, portfolio plan)
tells you *what* to learn. This set tells you *how to hold yourself to outside
standards* while doing the work inside a company that will not hold you to them.

## The risk this addresses

Two years of loose-practice work at a low-bar company can raise your internal
visibility while lowering your external ceiling. Symptoms:

- No tests or evals on shipped model/prompt changes.
- No design docs, no failure-mode analysis.
- No observability on AI systems in production.
- Leveling and comp decided informally, by politics and soft negotiation.

## How to use this set

1. Fill in `templates/scorecard.md` in week 1.
2. Follow `05-90-day-plan.md` for the first quarter.
3. Keep the Part 2 practices running permanently after that.
4. Re-score the checklist every quarter in `templates/scorecard.md`.

## Relationship to the other docs here

- `swe-fde-curriculum.md` and `portfolio-plan.md` define the *content* to study.
- This set defines the *discipline* that makes that content stick in a low-bar
  environment, and the measurement loop that tells you when you are actually
  market-ready.
- If a week in `05-90-day-plan.md` needs deeper study material, it points at the
  matching phase in `swe-fde-curriculum.md` or `portfolio-plan.md`.
